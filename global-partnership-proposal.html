Global Partnership Proposal

Together, reaching Africa with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

INTRODUCTORY DISCUSSION DOCUMENT

01 · Introduction

An invitation to explore collaboration

The Yet Africa Salvation For All invites churches, Christian ministries, mission organisations, foundations, businesses and individuals to explore purposeful collaboration in sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, encouraging churches and raising young evangelists to reach communities across Africa.

This document introduces the ministry's stated priorities and possible areas of partnership. It is a starting point for discussion, not a request for a particular amount of funding or a binding agreement.

02 · Ministry focus

Purpose and priorities

  • Sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ through evangelism and outreach.
  • Encouraging and supporting relationships with churches.
  • Raising and equipping young evangelists and emerging Christian leaders.
  • Building prayer, ministry and practical partnerships that can serve communities.
03 · Potential partnership areas

Ways to collaborate

Church and ministry partnerships

Explore joint prayer, discipleship, fellowship, ministry encouragement and appropriate outreach initiatives.

Mission and outreach support

Discuss support for a clearly scoped evangelism or community outreach activity, with an agreed plan, budget and reporting approach.

Youth and leadership development

Explore mentoring, training and practical development for young evangelists and emerging Christian leaders.

Resources, skills and prayer

Consider appropriate provision of Bibles, ministry materials, equipment, professional skills, training or ongoing prayer support.

04 · Suggested collaboration design

Agree the details together

For any proposed activity, the ministry and partner should jointly document:

  • The need or opportunity being addressed and the intended beneficiaries.
  • Specific activities, deliverables and realistic outcomes.
  • Roles, decision-making responsibilities and named contacts.
  • Timeline, locations and any relevant permissions.
  • Budget, approved payment arrangements and supporting records, if funding is involved.
  • Safeguarding, consent, privacy and risk-management measures appropriate to the activity.
  • Progress updates, financial reporting, review dates and how concerns will be raised.
05 · Accountability and stewardship

Responsible partnership principles

We believe partnership should be built on honesty, clear communication, mutual respect and responsible stewardship. Before any financial transfer or project launch, the parties should review relevant organisational documents, agree a written scope and budget, clarify authorised financial arrangements and establish how activities and resources will be reported.

Where children, young people or vulnerable people are involved, suitable safeguarding and consent arrangements should be agreed before activities begin. Partners should also disclose potential conflicts of interest and protect personal information.

Due diligence: This proposal does not claim independent audit status, formal accreditation, government registration details, quantified ministry impact or verified project outcomes. These items should be requested from the ministry and independently reviewed where relevant before a formal or funded partnership is approved.
06 · Proposed next steps

From introduction to partnership

  1. Partner submits an expression of interest and introductory information.
  2. Both parties hold an introductory conversation to understand shared priorities.
  3. Relevant organisational, safeguarding and financial documents are exchanged for due diligence.
  4. A specific activity or collaboration concept is jointly developed.
  5. Written responsibilities, budget, safeguards, timeline and reporting are agreed before implementation.
  6. Progress is reviewed and lessons are documented together.
