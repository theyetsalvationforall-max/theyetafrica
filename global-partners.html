Our invitation

One mission. Shared commitment. Practical collaboration.

The Yet Africa Salvation For All is a Christian evangelism ministry based in Uganda, with a vision to share the Gospel, encourage churches and raise young evangelists to reach communities across Africa.

We invite like-minded partners to begin a conversation about areas of shared purpose. We value relationships that respect local communities, encourage Christian service and are built on honesty, prayer, mutual understanding and responsible stewardship.

This programme is an invitation to explore partnership, not a representation that any specific project, funding arrangement or formal affiliation has already been agreed.

Areas of collaboration

Ways partners can contribute

The right partnership depends on shared priorities, local needs and the capacity of everyone involved.

Church and ministry relationships

Prayer, fellowship, discipleship, mutual encouragement and collaborative evangelism with churches and Christian ministries.

Mission and outreach

Explore support for agreed evangelism and community outreach activities, with defined objectives and reporting.

Youth and leadership

Discuss mentoring, practical training and development opportunities for young evangelists and emerging Christian leaders.

Resources and materials

Explore the responsible provision of Bibles, training materials, equipment and other resources linked to agreed ministry needs.

Prayer and advocacy

Build a network of prayer partners and advocates who encourage the ministry and its mission.

Knowledge and skills

Share professional skills, ministry experience, training support and advice where there is a clear mutual fit.

How we begin

A clear and respectful process

01

Register interest

Introduce yourself or your organisation and describe the kind of collaboration you would like to explore.

02

Conversation and review

The ministry can follow up using the contact details you provide to learn about shared priorities, capacity and questions.

03

Agree next steps

If both parties wish to proceed, document the scope, responsibilities, safeguards, budget and reporting before activities or transfers begin.
