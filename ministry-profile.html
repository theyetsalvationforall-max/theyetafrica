Who we are

The Yet Africa Salvation For All

The Yet Africa Salvation For All is a Christian evangelism ministry based in Uganda. The ministry's stated focus is sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, empowering and encouraging churches, and raising young evangelists to reach communities across Africa.

Our purpose

Mission and vision

Mission focus

To share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, encourage churches and equip young evangelists for outreach and Christian service.

Vision focus

To see communities across Africa reached with the hope of Christ and strengthened through faithful Christian witness.

Our areas of work

Ministry priorities

Evangelism and outreach

Sharing the Christian message and encouraging local engagement with communities.

Church encouragement

Building relationships that support churches and strengthen Christian fellowship.

Youth and evangelist development

Encouraging and equipping young people for evangelism and Christian leadership.

Prayer and partnership

Welcoming prayer, ministry relationships and practical collaboration with people and organisations who share the mission.

Working together

Partnership approach

We welcome conversations with churches, Christian ministries, mission organisations, foundations, businesses and individuals. Potential areas of collaboration include church partnerships, mission and outreach, youth development, resources, prayer and skills sharing.

We seek to approach partnerships through open communication, shared priorities, agreed responsibilities and responsible stewardship. A conversation or expression of interest does not in itself establish a formal partnership or funding commitment.
