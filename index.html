Who we are

Serving communities. Sharing the Gospel. Raising hope.

We are a Christian evangelism ministry committed to making the message of Jesus known and helping local churches and emerging leaders serve their communities.

Our heart is to see lives transformed by the love of Christ. We believe every community matters, every person deserves to hear the Good News, and young people can become powerful voices of faith and service.

“Go into all the world and preach the gospel to all creation.”
MARK 16:15
Our purpose

Mission, vision & values

Grounded in the Gospel and committed to practical, compassionate service.

Our missionTo spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ, empower churches, and equip young evangelists to reach people and communities across Africa.
Our visionA continent where communities are reached with the hope of Christ and local believers are equipped to share their faith.
Our valuesChrist-centred faith, love for people, integrity, unity, service, and the courage to share the Good News.
