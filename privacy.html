Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Yet Africa Salvation For All respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This policy explains the information that may be handled when you use the site.

Information you provide

If you use the Get Started form, the details you enter are used to prepare a message that opens in WhatsApp. The website itself does not submit or store that form information on a ministry server. You choose whether to send the message through WhatsApp.

If you contact the ministry by email, WhatsApp, mobile money or another service, the information you provide is handled by the relevant service and the ministry for the purpose of responding or processing your request.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

This website currently does not intentionally set its own advertising cookies or use a built-in analytics tracker. GitHub Pages, Cloudinary (if used for media), WhatsApp, email providers and other external services may process technical data under their own privacy policies.

Donations

Do not send passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes to anyone. Verify the recipient and transaction details before making a donation. Payment providers may process information under their own policies.

Children

Parents or guardians should guide children when they use contact forms or external communication services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact theyetsalvationforall@gmail.com.

This policy should be reviewed by the ministry and updated if the website begins collecting personal information, using analytics, or processing payments directly.
