INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIP INVITATION

Greetings in the name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

Dear Church Leader, Ministry Leader, Organisation Representative or Friend,

Warm Christian greetings from The Yet Africa Salvation For All.

We are grateful for your interest in our ministry and in the possibility of working together. We believe that sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and serving communities are responsibilities that call for prayer, faithfulness, compassion and partnership.

Our ministry's focus is to share the Gospel, encourage churches and raise young evangelists to reach communities across Africa. We recognise that each church and organisation brings its own calling, experience, gifts and resources. We therefore welcome respectful conversations about areas where our priorities may align.

Possible areas of collaboration include church and ministry relationships, evangelism and outreach, youth and leadership development, ministry resources, prayer and the sharing of skills and experience. Any specific activity would be discussed openly, with clear expectations, agreed responsibilities and appropriate accountability before it begins.

We would be pleased to learn more about your organisation, the work you are doing and the kind of partnership you have in mind. An initial conversation does not create a formal partnership or financial commitment; it simply gives us an opportunity to listen, pray and consider whether there is a suitable way forward.

Thank you for considering a connection with The Yet Africa Salvation For All. May the Lord guide our steps and strengthen every effort made in service to His Kingdom.

Yours in Christ,

The Yet Africa Salvation For All
